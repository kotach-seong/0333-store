# The manager's call before each shift (00:00) — English.
# Same layout as PhoneCalls.txt: the keys (점장, 수칙, 낙서) stay in Korean.
#   점장: line      one dialogue box
#   수칙: rule      written into the notebook's rules page, numbered
#   낙서: scribble  written into the notebook without a number

[DAY 1]
점장: Hello? You're the new night shift, right? I'm the manager.
점장: It's your first night, so just remember two things.
점장: One. Look carefully at what customers put on the counter.
점장: If there's anything mixed in that we don't sell... don't take it.
점장: Two. Check the CCTV once for every customer you ring up.
점장: If the customer in front of you isn't on the screen,
점장: or the one on screen looks different from what you see,
점장: or something that shouldn't be there shows up behind them or outside,
점장: mark "No" on the checklist and send them off. Don't talk to them.
점장: And... 3:33 in the morning.
점장: Whatever comes to the counter then, never close it as "Normal."
점장: It's always "No." Don't stall.
점장: I'll write the rules in your notebook. Your relief comes at 6. Just hold out till then.
수칙: Only ring up a customer once they're at the counter.
수칙: Do not approve if anything on the counter isn't from our store.
수칙: Do not approve if the receipt and CCTV show a different number of people.
수칙: Do not approve if the customer on CCTV differs from the one in front of you.
수칙: Never close the 3:33 a.m. transaction as Normal.

[DAY 2]
점장: Good work yesterday. ...Nothing happened, right?
점장: Starting today, you check the receipt too.
점장: The receipt total and the amount on the POS screen. They have to match.
점장: Sometimes they don't. It's not the machine. Don't take those.
수칙: Do not approve if the receipt total differs from the POS screen.

[DAY 3]
점장: ...It's me. Today, it's the time.
점장: Check that the time printed on the receipt matches the clock.
점장: If it shows a time that hasn't come yet, or one long gone,
점장: that customer shouldn't be here right now.
점장: Don't take it. Got it?
수칙: Do not approve transactions from the future or the past.

[DAY 4]
점장: Getting used to it? ...You sound tired.
점장: There's a line at the top of the receipt describing the customer.
점장: Check that it matches the person standing in front of you.
점장: A man printed as a woman, or the letters all garbled.
점장: And the receipt number. It has to go in order.
점장: If it repeats or skips, that's no good either.
점장: ...The one before you didn't follow this.
수칙: Do not approve if the receipt's customer differs from the one in front of you.
수칙: Do not approve if the receipt number repeats or skips.

[DAY 5]
점장: This is the last thing I'll teach you. Listen carefully.
점장: The receipt can't list anything that wasn't on the counter.
점장: Or any strange name you've never seen before.
점장: If they hand you something weird instead of money... that's not money. You know that.
점장: And if there's blood on the receipt, that goes without saying.
점장: Now you know everything I know.
수칙: Do not approve if the receipt lists items that weren't on the counter.
수칙: Do not approve if you're given something that isn't money.
수칙: Do not approve a receipt with blood on it.

[DAY 6]
점장: ...Hello? You're still there, right?
점장: Strange. I went through yesterday's CCTV... you weren't alone.
점장: (static) ...No. I must have seen wrong.
점장: Just follow the rules. Just follow the rules. Just the rules, just the rules, just the rules—
점장: (click)
낙서: Just follow the rules

[DAY 7]
점장: ......
점장: (breathing)
점장: Whoever comes at 6... look them straight in the face.
점장: I worked there for 7 days too.
점장: (click)
